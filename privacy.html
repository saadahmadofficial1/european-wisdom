Privacy Policy

European Wisdom · Last updated 18 October 2026

Who uses this app

The European Wisdom app is used only by the owner of the European Wisdom YouTube channel to manage that channel.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read basic channel and video information (scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No other copy is kept.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app does not collect any data about viewers.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
